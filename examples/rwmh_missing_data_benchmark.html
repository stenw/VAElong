Missing-Data Simulation Benchmark

VAE with RWMH vs VAE with Latent Sampling vs RNN vs Ordinary Mixed Model

Overview

This simulation benchmark focuses on one question:

How does a longitudinal VAE perform when it uses either the RWMH missing-data sampler or the Algorithm 1 latent-space sampler during training, compared with:

  • a direct Seq2Seq RNN
  • an ordinary linear mixed model benchmark

The workflow mirrors the newer EMA analysis more closely than the older simulation notebooks:

  • a train/validation/test split
  • validation-driven VAE selection
  • held-out test-set evaluation only
  • comparison on the future part of the trajectory after a landmark time

What the benchmark does

The Python script:

  • generates mixed-type longitudinal data with baseline covariates
  • injects random missingness
  • fits a VAE with imputation_method="rwmh"
  • fits a VAE with imputation_method="latent"
  • fits a Seq2Seq RNN benchmark
  • fits an ordinary mixed model benchmark
  • evaluates all four on the held-out future portion
  • saves training curves, profile plots, and metric summaries

The script lives at:

  • examples/rwmh_missing_data_benchmark.py

Notes

  • The benchmark now compares both supported VAE missing-data samplers.
  • The benchmark is intentionally narrower than the older mixed-type example: it focuses on the four-way comparison most relevant to the missing-data question instead of comparing many architectures at once.
